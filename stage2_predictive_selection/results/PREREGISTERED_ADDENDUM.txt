1. Over-squashing test (oversquash_test.py), implementation details
   - "Useful" pairs for the secondary rule are eligible pairs (at least 3 hops, not directly
     linked) with POSITIVE held-out ridge gain. Ridge gain uses the same set-up as the
     replication's selector, computed for every eligible pair on training data only.
   - Matching is one-to-one and greedy: low-resistance pairs in order of decreasing gain, each
     matched to the unused high-resistance pair at the same hop distance with the closest gain,
     accepted only if the gains differ by at most 20% of the larger one.
   - A same-target matching (both pairs share the target road) is reported as a robustness check.
     It does not decide the rule.
   - Swap fallback: if no training-period week has all 12 source readings observed, the nearest
     training-period week is used anyway; the percentage of such swaps is reported.
   - Day bootstrap: 1,000 resamples of whole test days, fixed random seed 12345.
   - The --test-windows option is for quick checks only and cannot be used for any decision.
   - Known imbalance for the primary rule: random_matched links sit much closer to their targets
     (median resistance percentile 30 on METR-LA) than express_links (89). The primary rule
     (A - B) therefore compares distant-source use with nearby-source use; it is reported as
     planned but interpreted with this caveat. The secondary rule is not affected.

2. Second model (run_other_model.py): is the effect Graph WaveNet's or the method's?
   Model: DCGRU, a simplified DCRNN-style diffusion-convolution GRU encoder (2 layers, 64 units,
   diffusion order 2 over the same forward/backward transition matrices, linear 12-step head),
   trained with the same data, batching, loss, optimiser, clipping, 100 epochs and best-validation
   checkpoint, on the same FROZEN link graphs (no re-selection) and evaluated on the identical
   test slice (same evaluation_sha256). METR-LA; original, express_links, forecast_only,
   random_matched; seeds 0, 1, 2.
   Decision (target_low_mae60, i.e. 60-minute MAE on slow readings of the 25 target roads):
   - The forecast-gain effect is NOT specific to Graph WaveNet if, with DCGRU, forecast_only is
     lower than random_matched on at least 2 of 3 seeds AND its 3-seed mean is lower than
     original's.
   - The same test is applied to express_links and reported alongside.
   - If neither holds, the effect is reported as specific to Graph WaveNet (or not robust).
