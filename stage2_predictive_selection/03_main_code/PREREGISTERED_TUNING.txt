PRE-REGISTRATION: tuning the Forecast-only link selector
Written 23 Nov 2026, before any tuning result. METR-LA, Graph WaveNet.
Revised 23 Nov 2026 (before any grid result was seen): inner-model preprocessing,
locked test plan, explicit fallback, narrower test-data wording.
The rules below are applied by tune_forecast_only.py exactly as written.

Question
Can the Forecast-only selector give larger gains on low-speed 60-minute forecasts if we
change how close the linked roads may be, how many links are added, how strongly they
are weighted, and which roads receive them?

Fixed throughout
- Data, strict split, gap-filling, scaler, batching and Graph WaveNet training: as in the
  replication (run_replication.py, unchanged). 100 epochs, best-validation checkpoint.
- Candidate links and scores: run_replication.predictive_scores, unchanged (16-link
  shortlist per road by fit-block gain, ranked by held-out gain on the last 20% of
  training). Only links with positive held-out gain are used. No self-links, no link
  that already exists in that direction.
- Low-speed cutoff: the Band 1 cutoff derived from training data.
- Weight 1x = median weight of the original road graph (0.3203 on METR-LA).
- The current Forecast-only (3 hops, 25 links, 1x weight) must be rebuilt exactly; the
  code stops if its graph differs from the frozen replication graph.

Metrics (60 minutes ahead)
- Main: low-speed MAE across all roads (Band 1).
- Also reported: overall MAE across all roads; low-speed MAE on the original 25 target roads.

Stage 1 - grid (validation only, seeds 0 and 1, averaged before ranking)
Minimum hops 3, 2, 1 x 10, 25, 50, 100 links, one link per road, 1x weight.
Hops 1 allows a reverse link to a directly connected road as well as longer links.
The original graph is trained as a reference only.
Eligible: mean validation overall MAE at most 0.01 mph above current Forecast-only.
The two eligible set-ups with the lowest mean validation Band 1 MAE go to the weight test.

Stage 1b - weights (validation only, seeds 0 and 1)
0.5x and 2x weight on those two set-ups.
Winner: among all eligible grid and weight set-ups, the lowest mean validation Band 1 MAE.
It must beat current Forecast-only by at least 0.02 mph; otherwise the winner is the
current Forecast-only. Both thresholds are applied to unrounded means.

Stage 3 - allocation (validation only, seeds 0 and 1)
Same hops, budget and weight as the winner; up to 3 links per road in both versions.
- Standard: links taken in order of held-out gain.
- Error-based: roads ranked by the original model's low-speed 60-minute MAE on the last
  20% of training (the selector's own inner holdout), averaged over seeds 0-2 of an
  original-graph model trained only on the first 80% of training. Its preprocessing
  (gap-fill medians, input scaler, low-speed cutoff) is fitted on the first 80% only,
  so the ranking period never informs it. Its checkpoint is chosen on the inner holdout;
  outer validation and test are not used. Outer validation judges the allocation.
  Roads with fewer than 30 low-speed readings or fewer than 3 days in that holdout are
  ranked last. Hardest roads first, each gets its best positive-gain links, up to 3,
  until the budget is used.
Error-based is adopted if its mean validation Band 1 MAE is at least 0.02 mph lower than
both the winner and standard allocation, and its overall MAE passes the 0.01 limit.
Otherwise the final set-up is the Stage 1 winner.

Stage 2 - confirmation (test split opened once, after all decisions above are saved)
Seeds 0-4 of: original graph, current Forecast-only, the final set-up, and links
randomly matched to the final set-up (same target roads, random eligible sources under
the same hop limit, fixed random seed 0). Finished replication runs of the same graph
and seed are reused instead of retrained.
Reported: mean of seeds, per-seed differences, and paired 95% intervals from a
day-block bootstrap over test days (10,000 draws, seed 20261123), for all three metrics.

Test plan lock
Before any test prediction, `final` saves the final set-up, the comparison set-ups, their
graph fingerprints, the checkpoint fingerprints of every seed, the seeds, the thresholds,
this rule and fingerprints of all stage decisions. An interrupted run resumes only if all of
these still match; otherwise it refuses to score. The verdict is saved with the results.

Test criterion (decides whether to carry the settings to DCRNN on PEMS-BAY)
If a tuned set-up won on validation, it passes if, on test Band 1 MAE pooled over seeds
0-4, the upper end of the paired 95% interval is below 0 both against current
Forecast-only and against its matched random links, and mean overall test MAE is at most
0.01 mph above current Forecast-only.
If no tuned set-up won on validation, the result is recorded as "no tuned improvement".
Whether current Forecast-only beats its own matched random links (upper end of the paired
95% interval below 0) is reported as a separate yes/no.

Test data
No test predictions or forecast scores are made before `final`. The shared data audit,
which runs in every stage, counts missing values and speed bands in all splits, including
test; it does not use any model.

Caveat
The METR-LA test split was used in earlier experiments, so this is a single pre-planned
look at a split already seen. The PEMS-BAY run with DCRNN, with links recomputed from
PEMS-BAY training data and the same settings, is a cross-dataset and cross-model
transfer check; PEMS-BAY also appeared in earlier experiments.

LargeST-GLA stays a separate experiment for the over-squashing question.
