ADDENDUM 2 to PREREGISTERED_DIAGNOSTIC.txt
Written 21 Nov 2026, before any DCRNN result was produced.

Second model changed from the home-made DCGRU to the published DCRNN.
Reason: a model taken from a trusted, citable source is a stronger test of whether the
forecast-only effect belongs to Graph WaveNet or to the link-selection method.

Model: DCRNN (Li et al., ICLR 2018), PyTorch code linked from the official repository
(github.com/chnsh/DCRNN_PyTorch, commit d92490b808ba5c5be2f23d427d96e9a56b066d7f), used
unchanged with the settings in its own config files. Data pipeline, frozen link graphs,
batching, checkpoint selection and evaluation are the same as for Graph WaveNet
(see README_dcrnn.md). METR-LA; original, express_links, forecast_only, random_matched;
seeds 0, 1, 2.

The decision rule from Addendum 1, section 2 is applied unchanged:
- The forecast-gain effect is NOT specific to Graph WaveNet if, with DCRNN, forecast_only is
  lower than random_matched on target_low_mae60 on at least 2 of 3 seeds AND its 3-seed mean
  is lower than original's.
- The same test is applied to express_links and reported alongside.
- If neither holds, the effect is reported as specific to Graph WaveNet (or not robust).

DCGRU results: had not been looked at when this addendum was written.
If they finish, they are reported as a supplementary check with a simplified model.
Note added 24 Nov 2026: the DCGRU jobs (61264-61266) were submitted automatically by submit_diagnostics.sh and finished, but their results had not been looked at when switching to DCRNN. They are reported as a supplementary check.
